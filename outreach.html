I-MRSEC brings real materials science research to local schools — from microscopes to Arduino lasers to VR molecules.

Program 1

Creative Materials Program
Franklin STEAM Academy

The I-MRSEC has an annual outreach program at local public middle school Franklin STEAM Academy. Over 7–8 weeks, 7th and 8th grade classes receive visits from I-MRSEC faculty, postdocs, and students several times per week — bringing engaging, hands-on lessons in materials science.

Interspersed with the visits are "creative" days where students express materials science concepts through the arts: drawing, comics, and poetry. The program also includes a field trip to the Materials Research Lab on the Illinois campus.

Program 2

IMAGINE STEM

IMAGINE STEM is another I-MRSEC outreach initiative that introduces young students to the excitement of materials research. Through interactive demos, coding activities, and lab visits, students discover how everyday materials are engineered at the atomic level.
